# How to second a nomination

Each election has a **Number of Required Nominations** setting, which is the number of people who must nominate a person for a position before that person becomes an eligible candidate. Each additional nomination for the same person and position is called a **second**.

If a nomination still needs more people to second it, it is shown with a **Need Second** button while nominations are open.

1. Go to **Elections**.
2. Go to **View And Submit Nomination** for the election.
3. Find the nomination that needs a second, and click **Need Second**.
4. Enter the reason why you want to second this nomination, then click **Nominate**.

Once a nomination has received the required number of nominations, the nominee becomes an eligible candidate and can [accept the nomination](user_accept_nomination.md). No further seconds are needed or accepted after that point.

If the **Number of Required Nominations** for the election is set to 1, then a single nomination is enough to make the nominee an eligible candidate, and seconding is not required.
