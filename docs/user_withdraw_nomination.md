# How to decline or withdraw a nomination

If you have been nominated for a position, you can say no at any time before **Voting opens**. Before you have accepted this is **Decline**, and the nomination is shown as **Declined**. After you have accepted (when you are a candidate) it is **Withdraw**, and the nomination is shown as **Withdrawn**. Either way, the person cannot be nominated again for the same position.

1. Go to **Elections**.
2. Open the election, or use the link in the email you were sent.
3. Under **Your nominations**, find your nomination, and select **Decline** (or **Withdraw**, if you have accepted). You can also select **Decline** on the **Accept Nomination** page.
4. The page is titled **Decline Nomination** (or **Withdraw Nomination**). Optionally enter **Comments** explaining why, then select **Decline** (or **Withdraw**). The message "Nomination declined." (or "Nomination withdrawn.") is shown. **Back to the election** above the heading leaves the page without answering.

A declined or withdrawn nomination is not counted towards the **Required nominations** and will not appear as a candidate when voting starts, even if it had already been [seconded](user_second_nomination.md) or [accepted](user_accept_nomination.md). People who view the election can see your comment.

Declining or withdrawing is not available once **Voting opens** has passed (except that a person written in by a voter can decline until voting closes - see [Write-ins](admin_write_ins.md)).
