# How to withdraw a nomination

If you have been nominated for a position, you can withdraw the nomination at any time before **Voting Start Date**. Once withdrawn, a nomination cannot be nominated again for the same position.

1. Go to **Elections**.
2. Go to **View And Submit Nomination** for the election, or the nomination confirmation page you were sent.
3. Find your nomination, and click **Withdraw Nomination**.
4. Optionally enter comments explaining why you are withdrawing, then click **Withdraw**.

A withdrawn nomination is not counted towards the **Number of Required Nominations** and will not appear as a candidate when voting starts, even if it had already been [seconded](user_second_nomination.md) or [accepted](user_accept_nomination.md).

Withdrawing a nomination is not available once **Voting Start Date** has passed.
