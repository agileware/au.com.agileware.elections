# How to duplicate an election

Duplicate an election to run it again, for example next year's election, without entering everything again.

1. Go to **Elections** and find the election in the list.
2. Click **Duplicate**.
3. Enter the **name of the copy** (it starts as "Copy of ..."), and the **start date of the copy**.
4. Click **Duplicate**.

The copy keeps the original's settings (required nominations, who can take part, voting method, online nominations, emails, revoting, anonymising, non-logged in access) and display options, and all of its positions in the same order. **All dates move by the same amount**, so the stages keep their spacing relative to the new start date. Nominations, candidates, votes and results are **not** copied.

The copy is created **inactive**. Check its dates and positions, then [activate it](admin_activate_election.md).

A single position can also be duplicated - see [How to add a position](admin_add_position.md).
