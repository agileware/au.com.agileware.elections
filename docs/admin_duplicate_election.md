# How to duplicate an election

Duplicate an election to run it again, for example next year's election, without entering everything again.

1. Go to **Elections** and find the election in the list.
2. Select **Duplicate** on the election's row. A **Duplicate Election** dialog opens.
3. Enter the **Name of the copy** (it starts as "Copy of" followed by the original name), and **Visible from** for the copy.
4. Select **Duplicate**, or **Cancel** to close the dialog.

The copy keeps the original's settings (required nominations, who can take part, voting method, online nominations, emails, revoting, anonymising, taking part without logging in) and display options, and all of its positions in the same order. **All dates move by the same amount**, so the stages keep their spacing relative to the new **Visible from** date. Nominations, candidates, votes and results are **not** copied.

The copy is added **inactive**, and the message "Election duplicated. It is inactive until you activate it." is shown. Check its dates and positions, then [activate it](admin_activate_election.md).

A single position can also be duplicated - see [How to add a position](admin_add_position.md).
