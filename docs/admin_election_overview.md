# Election overview

Select an election's name in [Manage Elections](admin_manage_elections.md) to see it at a glance:

- **Stage**: Inactive, Not started, Nominations open, Nominations closed, Candidates advertised, Voting open, Voting closed or Results published.
- **Next**: what to do next, in plain words, for example "Add at least one position, then activate the election so people can see it."
- **Timeline**: every date of the election, with the ones that have passed ticked.
- **Numbers**: positions, nominations, eligible candidates, candidates standing (those who accepted) and ballots cast.
- Shortcuts to the election's **Positions**, **Candidates**, **Edit settings** (only while the election can still be edited), **View as a voter** and **Duplicate**.
- **Turnout** (once voting has started): the ballots cast out of the people allowed to vote, and for each position how many candidates are standing and how many ballots ranked someone for it. Only counts are shown, never who voted for whom. When the election's votes are anonymised, the number of ballots comes from the Vote activities and the count for each position is not shown.
- **Results** (once the results are calculated): the final ranking for each position, as a table you can sort and search.
