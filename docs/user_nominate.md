# How to nominate a user

You can nominate yourself, an existing nominees or a new person. Each nominee must be a permitted contact in CiviCRM (as defined in the Elections Settings).

1. Go to **Elections**  
![Click Elections](images/user_nominate/01.gif)  
2. Select **Nominate** on the election's card (the button says **Nominate** while nominations are open and you can nominate), then **Nominate** in the "what you can do now" panel of the election page  
![Click Vote in the election](images/user_nominate/02.gif)     
3. Enter information on the **Nominate in** (election name) page:
- **Position**: Choose the position that you want to nominate the user in ("Select a position")  
- **Nominee**: Choose the name of the person that you want to nominate ("Select a person"). Type a name or email to search.  
- **Reason for the nomination**: Enter the reason why you want to nominate this person  
![Fill the nomination form](images/user_nominate/03.gif)
4. Select **Nominate** to nominate. The message "Nomination submitted." is shown. **Back to the election** above the heading leaves the form without nominating.
![Click Nominate](images/user_nominate/04.gif)

Depending on the election's settings, a nomination may need to be seconded by one or more other people before the nominee becomes an eligible candidate - see [How to second a nomination](user_second_nomination.md). The nominee can [decline or withdraw the nomination](user_withdraw_nomination.md) at any time before voting starts.
