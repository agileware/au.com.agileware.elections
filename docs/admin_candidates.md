# Candidates

**Candidates** for an election (open it from [Manage Elections](admin_manage_elections.md)) lists everyone nominated, role by role:

- **Candidate** (select the name to see their candidate page), the **position**, and how many people nominated them (**Nominators**).
- **Eligible**: they have enough nominations to be a candidate (see **Number of Required Nominations** in [How to create a new election](admin_create_election.md)).
- **Accepted**: the nominee has accepted, so they appear on the ballot.
- **Withdrawn**: the nominee withdrew.
- The candidate's **Statement** and **Statement link**.

Select a statement, a link or a Yes/No value to change it in place, then confirm with the tick.

- Who is standing (Eligible, Accepted, Withdrawn) can be changed until **voting starts**. After that it is fixed, so the ballot cannot change while people are voting.
- Statements and links can be corrected until **voting ends**.

## Add Candidate

Use **Add Candidate** when nominations are made outside the website, for example by a board. Choose the position and the person, optionally enter their statement, and tick **The candidate has accepted** if they have already agreed to stand, so they appear on the ballot. The person must belong to one of the election's groups; add them to a group first if they do not. Candidates can be added until voting starts.
