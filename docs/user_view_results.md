# How to view election results

Once the election results have been published, then this information is available to the public.

1. Go to **Elections**  
![Click Elections](images/user_view_results/01.gif)    
2. Select **View Results** on the election's card  
![Click Vote in the election](images/user_view_results/02.gif)       
3. If you want to see the whole process of the election, select **View Election Summary** (**Back to the election** returns to the results)  
![Click Nominate](images/user_view_results/03.gif)  

The results page is titled "Results for" followed by the election's name, and shows **Election Results**: the **Winner for** each position (**Winners for** when it has more than one seat), each with a **Winner** badge. Each position's results show a status describing how the result was reached:

- **Winner by clear majority**: the winning candidate(s) received an outright majority of votes once preferences were distributed.
- **Elected by the most votes**: (elections counted by most votes) the candidates with the most votes fill the seats.
- **The result was a tie...**: the candidates were level across the last seat, so every tied candidate is listed with a **Tied** badge and none is chosen over the others. Candidates who clearly won keep the **Winner** badge.
- **The number of candidates equals the number of seats**: all of the candidates are elected.
- **Not all seats were filled for this position**: there were not enough eligible candidates to fill every seat for the position.
- **There are not enough candidates for this position to generate a result**: no candidates had accepted their nomination for this position.

The election summary page, titled "Election Summary for" followed by the election's name, lists for each position who was **Elected**, the **Candidates** and the **Nominations**.

## Questions

A yes or no question shows **Passed**, **Not passed** (including a tie) or **No quorum**, with the resolution. Counts are not shown.

The results page can have a logo, a header and a footer chosen by the organisers, and for a few days after the results are published it may show a short celebration (confetti, fireworks and so on). Press **Skip the celebration** or Escape to end it. Nothing is shown if your device asks for reduced motion.
