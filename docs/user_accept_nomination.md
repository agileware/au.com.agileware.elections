# Accepting a Nomination

Once a nomination has received the number of nominations required by the election's settings, the nominee becomes an **eligible candidate** - see [How to second a nomination](user_second_nomination.md). To become a candidate, an eligible candidate must then accept the nomination before **Voting opens**. Nominations are usually accepted while the election is at the **Candidates advertised** stage, after nominations have closed and while people wait to see who has accepted their nomination.

The nominee is emailed with a single **Accept Nomination** link (see [Emails and scheduled jobs](reference_emails_and_jobs.md)). The link opens the **Accept Nomination** page, which says "You have been nominated for" the position and shows the election and position. When accepting, the nominee writes a **Statement** for voters (their experience, what they want to achieve) and can add a **Statement link** to a longer statement or CV (it must start with http:// or https://). Select **Accept** to accept: the message "Nomination accepted." is shown. Select **Decline** instead if you do not want to stand (see [Decline or withdraw a nomination](user_withdraw_nomination.md)). **Back to the election** above the heading leaves the page without answering.

The statement is shown on the candidate's page and, depending on the election's [display options](admin_create_election.md), on the ballot. A candidate can change their statement, using **Edit Statement** on their candidate page, until voting starts. Election administrators can correct a statement at any time before voting ends.

Accepting is done by the nominee themselves. The election page shows **Your nominations**: each position you are nominated for, where it stands (*Waiting for 1 more nominator*, *Waiting for your answer*, *Accepted: you are a candidate*, *Declined* or *Withdrawn*) and the buttons you can use (**Accept Nomination** and **Decline** before you answer; **Edit Statement** and **Withdraw** after you accept). To use them, open the election while logged in as yourself (or through your personal link, if [taking part without logging in](setup.md#participating-without-logging-in) is enabled). An administrator cannot accept on someone's behalf, and some site tools that let an administrator impersonate a user (for example Drupal masquerade) may not work with CiviCRM sessions - log in as the nominee in a separate browser instead.

If the nominee does not accept a nomination, then they will not be listed as a candidate when voting starts, after the **Voting opens** date.

A nominee can instead [decline a nomination](user_withdraw_nomination.md) if they do not want to be a candidate for the position.

## If you were written in by a voter

If a voter writes you in during voting, you are emailed with an **Accept Nomination** link to accept or decline. You can answer until voting closes: if you accept, you become a candidate and appear on the ballot, and the votes already written in for you count; if you decline, or do not answer, those votes are dropped. You will get one reminder in the last day. Once you have accepted you cannot withdraw. See [Write-ins](admin_write_ins.md).

If you open the link after voting has closed you will see a message saying the opportunity to answer has passed and that you are not on the ballot, with a **Back to the election** button. If you never answered, you are also sent an email to say so. Nothing more is needed from you.
