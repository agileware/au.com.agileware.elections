# Accepting a Nomination

Once a nomination has received the number of nominations required by the election's settings, the nominee becomes an **eligible candidate** - see [How to second a nomination](user_second_nomination.md). To become a candidate, an eligible candidate must then accept the nomination before the **Voting Start Date**. Nominations are usually accepted during the **Advertise Candidates** stage, after nominations have closed and while people wait to see who has accepted their nomination.

When accepting, the nominee writes a **candidate statement** for voters (their experience, what they want to achieve) and can add a **link to a longer statement or CV**. The statement is shown on the candidate's page and, depending on the election's [display options](admin_create_election.md), on the ballot. A candidate can change their statement, using **Edit statement** on their candidate page, until voting starts. Election administrators can correct a statement at any time before voting ends.

Accepting is done by the nominee themselves. The election page shows **Your nominations**: each position you are nominated for, where it stands (*Waiting for 1 more nominator*, *Waiting for your answer*, *Accepted: you are a candidate* or *Withdrawn*) and the buttons you can use (**Accept Nomination**, **Withdraw Nomination**, **Edit statement**). To use them, open the election while logged in as yourself (or through your personalised link, if [non-logged in access](setup.md#participating-without-logging-in) is enabled). An administrator cannot accept on someone's behalf, and some site tools that let an administrator impersonate a user (for example Drupal masquerade) may not work with CiviCRM sessions - log in as the nominee in a separate browser instead.

If the nominee does not accept a nomination, then they will not be listed as a candidate when voting starts, after the **Voting Start Date**.

A nominee can instead [withdraw a nomination](user_withdraw_nomination.md) if they do not want to be a candidate for the position.

## If you were written in by a voter

If a voter writes you in during voting, you are emailed with links to accept or decline. You can answer until voting closes: if you accept, you become a candidate and appear on the ballot, and the votes already written in for you count; if you decline, or do not answer, those votes are dropped. You will get one reminder in the last day. Once you have accepted you cannot withdraw. See [Write-ins](admin_write_ins.md).

If you open the link after voting has closed you will see a message saying the opportunity to answer has passed and that you are not on the ballot. If you never answered, you are also sent an email to say so. Nothing more is needed from you.
